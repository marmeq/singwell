Privacy Policy

Singwell: Vocal Warm Up · Last updated October 29, 2026

Singwell helps you warm up your voice. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Data you enter

Your vocal range and warm-up history are stored only on your device in the app's local database. We do not operate servers that receive this data, there are no accounts, and we cannot see your entries.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no tracking.

Microphone

Singwell uses the microphone to detect the pitch of your voice in real time. Audio is analyzed on your device and is never uploaded. If you have Singwell Pro, the app can keep a recording of your latest warm-up on your device so you can listen back; it is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device. These are not sent through our servers.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Health

Singwell is a practice tool, not medical advice. If singing causes pain or hoarseness that doesn't go away, see a doctor or voice specialist.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Support & contact

Questions, feedback or problems: arseniiprokaev@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.
